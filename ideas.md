# Bobby's Pre-PT Landing Page - Design Brainstorm

## Response 1: Athletic Minimalism with Performance Focus
**Design Movement:** Swiss Design meets Athletic Branding (think Nike, Strava)
**Probability:** 0.08

**Core Principles:**
- Clean, geometric layouts with asymmetric grid structures
- Emphasis on data and performance metrics (running stats, clinical hours)
- Functional typography that communicates hierarchy and professionalism
- Negative space as a design tool to guide the eye

**Color Philosophy:**
- Deep navy (#1a2332) as primary—conveys trust, professionalism, and athletic intensity
- Vibrant teal accent (#00d4d4)—energy, movement, and clinical precision
- Warm cream (#f5f1e8) for breathing room and readability
- Strategic use of grayscale for data visualization

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Asymmetric grid with offset sections
- Hero section with diagonal cut using clip-path
- Staggered content blocks (text left, image right, then reversed)
- Floating cards for achievements and credentials

**Signature Elements:**
- Geometric dividers (triangles, angled cuts) between sections
- Subtle animated progress bars for skills/experience
- Minimalist icons from Lucide React
- Running/athletic motifs in subtle background patterns

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Smooth hover effects that reveal additional information
- Click-to-expand sections for credentials and experience
- Scroll-triggered animations that feel natural and purposeful
- CTA buttons with subtle scale and shadow transforms

**Animation:**
- Fade-in on scroll with staggered delays for content blocks
- Smooth transitions (300ms) for all interactive elements
- Subtle parallax on hero image for depth
- Animated counters for statistics (clinic hours, marathon training)

**Typography System:**
- Display: Poppins Bold (700) for headlines—geometric, modern, athletic
- Body: Inter Regular (400) for readability
- Accent: Poppins SemiBold (600) for subheadings
- Hierarchy: 3.5rem → 2rem → 1.25rem → 1rem

## Selected Design Approach: Athletic Minimalism with Performance Focus

I've chosen **Response 1** for your landing page. This approach perfectly aligns with your brand as a Pre-PT student, marathon runner, and future sports PT. Here's why:

- **Professional yet Athletic:** The Swiss design aesthetic conveys clinical credibility while the teal accent brings energy and movement
- **Performance-Focused:** The emphasis on metrics and data visualization will showcase your clinical hours, running achievements, and academic progress
- **Modern & Scalable:** Clean typography and asymmetric layouts create visual interest without feeling cluttered
- **Future-Ready:** This design foundation can grow with your practice and content

The navy + teal + cream palette is sophisticated, accessible, and distinctly yours—not generic. The geometric dividers and asymmetric layout will make your site stand out from typical student portfolios.
